Everything you need to know about CivicEye, AI verification, and municipal SLAs.

CivicEye runs every image through an AI multimodal authenticity filter that inspects EXIF camera metadata, capture timestamps, and optical anomalies. If a photo is an internet meme, stock image, or already reported duplicate, it is flagged instantly.
